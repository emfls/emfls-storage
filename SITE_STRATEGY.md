# Site Strategy

Site No.: 16
Repo: emfls-storage
Domain: https://storage.emfls.com/

The site-specific mission, topic boundary, target user, information architecture,
and growth strategy come from Site 16's current Notion Site Control Page.

## Foundation scope

Help Korean-speaking visitors organize storage through a repeatable sequence:
classify what is being stored, assign a place based on point of use and access
frequency, measure usable space before judging capacity, then make labels and
maintenance easy to repeat. The foundation page names the six Site Control areas
without expanding them into deep guides or a shopping catalog.

Keep storage-system advice distinct from moving logistics, living spaces,
interior styling, and food content. Avoid pressure to discard possessions.

This is the horizontal minimum Foundation/Search Launch slice. Deep guides,
advanced tools, and fine design polish remain deferred until the network-wide
Foundation/Search Launch pass is complete.
