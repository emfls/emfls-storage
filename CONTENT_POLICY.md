# Content Policy

The Foundation publishes only a small set of distinct site and Trust pages; it
does not attempt to complete the eventual guide library.

Rules for later stages:

- Follow the site's documented Topic Boundary.
- Do not mass-generate thin SEO pages.
- Do not invent factual claims or unsupported statistics.
- Preserve clear editorial and source standards.
- Avoid duplicate search intent across EMFLS sites.
- Do not invent contact details, product claims, statistics, endorsements, or
  storage capacities.
- Do not give unsafe chemical, medication, or other hazardous-storage
  instructions. Direct those cases to authoritative product labels and local
  safety guidance rather than guessing.
- Do not pressure visitors to discard belongings. Present keeping, relocating,
  donating, recycling, and holding as context-dependent choices.
- The Contact route points to public repository Issues; tell visitors that
  reports are public and should not contain personal or confidential data.
